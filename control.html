P, I and D in plain words

PThe push · the present

Pushes in proportion to the error right now. Bigger Kp is faster, but it always leaves a small gap.

IThe memory · the past

Adds up the error over time. As long as any gap is left, it keeps pushing harder, until the gap is zero.

DThe brake · the future

Looks at how fast the error is shrinking and eases off early, so the output does not shoot past the target.
