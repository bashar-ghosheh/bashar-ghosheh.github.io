Motor, sensor and controller equations

Motor

The normalized motor has unit gain and a 1 s time constant.

Sensor

The HW1 sensor measures wheel speed. At zero lag its reading equals the true speed.

PID controller

The motor command depends on the difference between target speed and sensor reading.

The simulation applies a unit step with unrestricted drive and advances the motor and sensor using RK4 at 1 ms intervals. With the reference gains, swings decay below 1 s of sensor lag, persist at 1 s, and grow above 1 s.
