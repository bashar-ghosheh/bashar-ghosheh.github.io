Tuning challenge

A plant you have never seen is on the bench. You get one noisy step test, a spec to meet and three gains. Read the plant, tune the loop, then test it against the spec.

How to play
  1. Read the plant. The bench shows the hidden plant's answer to a unit step, with no controller. Is it first or second order? Estimate its numbers, as in Guess the sensor.
  2. Tune. Set Kp, Ki and Kd. The closed loop stays hidden until you test.
  3. Test. The page simulates your loop on the true plant and marks each line of the spec PASS or FAIL. The first three tests are free, each later test costs 5% of the score.
  4. Reveal. See the true plant and one set of gains that works, with the working.

Want to explore first? Turn on Live preview: the curve follows the sliders, and the round becomes a practice round with no score.
